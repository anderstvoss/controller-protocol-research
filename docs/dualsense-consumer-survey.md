# DualSense — public software-consumer survey (G)

**Research parity, part 2: consumers and decisions** · **source-only**, not run. Evidence reference: research main after A–F merged; virtualgamepad main `8f60dcce577ab02bd8ed8b3426349729d417393c` was reviewed for the acceptance-model split, not modified. Physical controller identifier: `054c:0ce6`. Consumer inventory ranked by likely acceptance value, **not** probability of successful recognition. Rank does not mean supported.

**Realization semantics:** `linux.uinput` creates evdev nodes only; `linux.uhid.usb` creates a virtual HID bus-visible device with a descriptor which might be bound by `hid-playstation` or `hid-generic`, exposing hidraw and derived evdev. Neither represents a physical USB controller, RF or XUSB interface. `linux.uhid.bluetooth`, if declared, only presents BUS_BLUETOOTH metadata—not BlueZ pairing. `dummy_hcd`/USB/IP/composite audio is *not* interchangeable with UHID. Only an explicitly separate USB gadget/USB-IP composite-audio topology could expose USB Audio; ordinary UHID and uinput never provide UAC audio. No such live path validated.

**Family-specific sourced behavior:** SDL PS5 requests feature 0x09/0x20/0x05, parses USB report 0x01 and sends output 0x02; kernel hid-playstation probes feature/identity and drives FF/LED. Wine has explicit Sony BT hidraw fixups. Pinned claims and sources from part 1: `audio-linux-hid-playstation`, `audio-sdl-ps5`, `parity-g-wine-winebus`. No physical acceptance or exact Steam private reporting; reported dummy_hcd versus UHID Steam gyro remains hypothesis for association.
