# Decision brief — MotionPlus F7 challenge state and 0x50 response

**Recommendation to Anders:** **Keep the active MotionPlus F7 value in the bounded F6 downstream passthrough compatibility state fixed at `0x02`, and continue returning the existing bounded error 7 for challenge data at `0x50`, for the current *compatibility* F6 passthrough mirror. Do not advance F7 without serving coherent challenge data.** Promote a complete, independently implemented challenge only after a specific guest/application failure and owner-approved physical or high-confidence source evidence. This does **not** claim a faithful physical MotionPlus challenge. The owner decision to hold F7 at `0x02` (October 27, 2026) has a separate [merged W9 source-only record](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/074bfad23e8dfa7851540808228f0ce3efe1edc9/records/claim/wii-w9-f7-fixed-preparing-compat.json); it applies to the **active MP plus downstream Nunchuk/Classic F6 mirror**, not a default claim for every remote mode, and is **not** a new lab observation.

This is a **source-only research brief**, initiated from research main `bd1357c70eeeca4a91b29e34671765e6a05ea5c0` (166 Wii claims). During drafting, unrelated W9 F7 compatibility evidence merged as main `074bfad23e8dfa7851540808228f0ce3efe1edc9` (167 claims). These findings now add only `wii-gap-challenge-*` records **on top of W9**, without editing that record. The previous [Dolphin attachment findings](wii-dolphin-attachment.md) and [cross-consumer comparison](wii-motionplus-consumer-comparison.md) remain authoritative for F6 and `A40040`; this brief does not redefine those behaviors. **No tests below were run.**

| **3 — Do not implement alone** | **(b) Simulate `0x02 → 0x04 → 0x08 → 0x0c → 0x0e` then `0x14 → 0x18 → 0x1a`, without `0x50`** | Could let a poller stop waiting but **advertises ready data that is not supplied**. A client that then reads `0x50` receives error 7 or fails validation; no independently observed consumer benefits from this partial step. | Neither a complete **compat** solution nor hardware-faithful response; creates misleading state. **Do not confuse outcomes.** A guest reporting that MotionPlus is unsupported, unavailable, or rejected after a challenge does **not** show the rest of the game cannot launch or play without MotionPlus. Conversely, a game that boots or delivers buttons does **not** prove MotionPlus authentication or usable gyro. The outcome is game- and version-specific.

## Technical trace

### 1. Provenance: F7 `0x02` and the two stages

- **Independent community observation:** [WiiBrew revision `120079`](https://wiibrew.org/w/index.php?title=Wii_Motion_Plus&oldid=120079) reports inactive `A600F7` after MotionPlus initialization beginning at `0x02`, then `0x04`, `0x08`, `0x0c`, and `0x0e`. It also reports a changing **128-byte** span at inactive `A60050..A600CF`; the linked raw historical samples are not independently reproduced here. WiiBrew adds that **games do not need to read this initial F7 byte**, a narrower statement about its documented setup path. The F7 progression is a credible **hardware-derived, community-reported** observation (**moderate confidence** for the first stage, *not laboratory verified in this project*). Timing and integrated-remote applicability remain open.
- **Dolphin 2019 code history:** Dolphin's [April 23, 2019 cleanup commit `59e1c834`](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/59e1c83445bc869b3cf79e9461194faac7a64efe/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.h#L84-L105) already describes the F7 sequence and F1 second-stage steps; the [April 26 PR #7861 head](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/ba1b3351184864ed3d5692a461b00edd2f3a21f6/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.h#L58-L135) refines it into a `ChallengeState` enum and [F7 comments](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/ba1b3351184864ed3d5692a461b00edd2f3a21f6/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.h#L116-L130). The [pinned 2026 source](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.h#L166-L235) retains that description. The comment explicitly says the **emulator jumps** to ready states rather than reproducing all intermediate real-MP values. It therefore cannot itself serve as an independent real-hardware capture.
- **Second-stage `F1` evidence:** Dolphin describes a write of **0 or 1** to `F1` after first-stage `0x0e`, followed by `0x14 → 0x18 → 0x1a`. [Write dispatch](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L268-L300) and [register layout](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.h#L198-L231) implement the branch. The 2019 Dolphin developers give first-hand reverse-engineering context in [their contemporaneous account](https://dolphin-emu.org/blog/2019/04/26/mastering-motion/) and [PR #7861](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/pull/7861), but **no independently archived physical F1 timing capture was located** in the reviewed WiiBrew, wiiuse, libogc, Cemu, SDL, or kernel references. Classify this as **hardware-derived claim in Dolphin, low-to-moderate independent confidence**, not confirmed across external, early integrated, or TR revisions.
- **Bank distinction:** WiiBrew's first-stage examples concern **inactive `A600`** addresses, while the pinned Dolphin emulator uses the same `Register` layout under inactive `A600` and active `A400` access, depending on activation state. [Read routing](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L190-L224). Do **not** silently turn WiiBrew's inactive F7 observation into a measured active `A400F7` trace. Also distinguish internal Dolphin `Activating=0x00`, explicitly declared **not** a physical F7 value, from `PreparingX=0x02`. [State enum](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.h#L166-L179).

**Historical modification timeline:** community documentation of inactive startup progression predates the 2019 rebuilt emulator; [2010 legacy history](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/commit/58d64625f9feb020e56edd1be3316dac68f886ab) used fixed challenge-like payloads to get games running, not a fully understood exchange. [PR #7861](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/pull/7861) implemented the two-branch mathematical model after code review and crypto research. [PR #14910](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/pull/14910), observed **open/unmerged** on October 27, 2026, proposes replacing its existing deterministic big-number computation with precomputed challenge results, claiming no behavior change. This is a **proposal**, not evidence of a merged runtime or independent hardware measurement. [Pinned proposal code](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3503d8eac1a1fccf22f32dcdce81dbc46322559f/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L451-L525).

### 2. The `0x50` region, X/Y math and timing

| Window / operation | Dolphin's concrete implementation | Provenance boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F7 | State/readiness byte; represented in emulator as activation placeholder `0x00`, `PreparingX=0x02`, `XReady=0x0e`, `PreparingY=0x14`, `YReady=0x1a`. [enum](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.h#L166-L179) | Real intermediate `0x04/0x08/0x0c` and `0x18` **not simulated**; real behavior is described in comments, and first stage corroborated only by WiiBrew. |
| `0x50..0x8f` | Active or inactive MotionPlus emulated register contains a **64-byte little-endian** big-integer slot overwritten with **parameter X**, **Y0**, or **Y1**. [layout](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.h#L194-L202), [generator](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L481-L522) | WiiBrew separately reports **128 changing bytes** `A60050..CF`. The claimed width/region differ; do not infer the remaining `0x90..cf` is meaningful crypto data or that all revisions share one precise layout. |
| X stage | Dolphin chooses a fixed reusable input (real hardware reportedly varies each run), computes a modular square under a common modulus, returns a 64-byte X value, marks F7 ready `0x0e`. [calculation](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L465-L497) | Emulator's input is deliberately **deterministic**, not an OEM entropy/revision model. |
| F1 / Y selection | Game writes `0` or `1` to challenge-type F1 once X is ready. Dolphin produces Y0 as the original chosen integer, or Y1 as its product with a published derived square-root factor, reduced modulo the same modulus; 64 bytes, little-endian. [F1 handler](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L268-L299), [Y stage](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L499-L521) | The 2019 developers classify the construction as **Rabin-type public-key math**. We intentionally omit all modulus, factor, secret, identifier, output and replay bytes. |
| Timing | Dolphin delays activation about **20 ms**, X readiness about **500 ms** (it notes real ~**1500 ms**), and Y0/Y1 by about **30/500 ms**, respectively (its comment estimates real Y1 ~**1200 ms**). It documents SDK/game timeout budgets near **one minute for X** and around **0.5 or 9.5 seconds** for Y choices. [activation](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L342-L361), [X](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L395-L412), [Y](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L270-L296) | These are **Dolphin estimates and intentional compatibility timing shortcuts**, not measurements by this task; no exact physical deadline is asserted. |

**Mathematics and rights:** The Rabin method and the high-level modular-square / two-root construction are published mathematical ideas. Dolphin's [April 2019 account](https://dolphin-emu.org/blog/2019/04/26/mastering-motion/) publicly discusses the factorization research (flacs, degasus and others) and two challenge branches. In [pinned source](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L465-L522), Dolphin uses **mbedTLS multiprecision arithmetic** with shared mathematical parameters. It does **not** read a controller-unique secret or require a per-console private key in the reviewed emulated path. The historically derived parameters and any challenge outputs are **not included here**. Implementing an original algorithm from mathematics does **not inherently require copying GPL code**. Directly porting Dolphin's implementation or data may trigger its **GPL-2.0-or-later** obligations and provenance review, regardless of the algorithm's mathematical openness. An independent implementation would need externally validated parameters/test oracle in an appropriately controlled, non-published owner-owned resource or a documented safe provenance path, subject to project policy. This PR does **not** authorize code transplantation, key export or release of static challenge outputs.

**Stuck at `0x02`:** A client that **only reads F6/F8/F9 or gyroscope data** is unaffected by this value. A client that requires **F7 `0x0e` before X** likely waits, times out, and treats MP as uninitialized. A client that ignores F7 but reads `0x50` receives the current error 7. A client that uses a fallback may run *without MotionPlus*; whether that happens is a per-title observation, not guaranteed. The absence of a failure in Dolphin MP-only ControllerInterface W8 is **not** a Wii game authentication test. WiiBrew's phrase that games do not need to read first-stage inactive F7 and Dolphin's statement that games read F7 to check challenge readiness refer to **different described code paths/stages**; do not force them into a physical contradiction or reciprocal `conflicts_with`.

### 4. Decisive physical capture — owner authorization required (**not run**)

**Minimum hardware:** one known **external MotionPlus** and one original Nunchuk with an owner-supplied real Wii Remote, exclusive debug/probe access, reversible setup and sanitized local capture. Repeat as *separate revision-scoped studies* for **early integrated Wii Remote Plus** and **TR**; never infer interchangeability from a single unit.

1. **Preflight / baseline:** log revision, active/inactive mode, report flags, relative monotonic time and permitted outcomes. With MP inactive, initialize downstream accessory and read its normal **A400FA/6** ID and **A40020/16** calibration (private); read inactive MP **A600FA/6** and **A600F7/1** at startup. Do not publish ID/calibration bytes.
2. **First-stage F7 time series:** write ordinary init **A600F0=55**, poll **A600F7/1** promptly at controlled short intervals through at least the expected ~**1.5-second** preparation period (and a bounded longer check if no ready state). Capture timestamped state values and errors; sample **A60050** only at preselected start/ready points using bounded reads, reducing data to length/presence/change flags and salted session-local digest, not raw challenge bytes.
3. **Activate mode 05:** write **A600FE=05**, record status/ACK and any transition gap, poll **A400F7/1** after activation. Read active **A400FA/6**, **A400F6/4** (observe F7 byte within that 4-byte read), and **A40040/16**. Compare F6 identity field correspondence and 40 calibration equality to *the same accessory's inactive* FA/20 data using only boolean comparisons. This extends [PR #46](wii-dolphin-attachment.md), not a separate invented hardware claim.
4. **Challenge X and Y branches:** on a fresh activation for each branch, wait for the X-ready indication if any, read bounded **`0x50`** bytes within the correct bank; then write **F1=0** on one fresh cycle and **F1=1** on a separate fresh cycle. Poll F7 and read the corresponding `0x50` only at measured ready states. Keep untried writes and retries bounded; verify restore/normal MP reporting. Record whether the register is readable, length, stable window, time-to-ready, and validator acceptance *if an authorized non-secret oracle exists*.
5. **Controls and uncertainty:** record a no-challenge consumer baseline, one game/SDK probe *only if owner supplies lawful test environment*, timing and timeout outcomes. Disambiguate **game rejects MotionPlus** from **game launches without MotionPlus**. Do not run any of these tests in this research-only task.

**Safe publication:** reduced chronological state labels, register offsets, read lengths, error classes, timing distributions, boolean equality of inactive-vs-active identity/calibration, consumer outcomes and provenance. Do **not** publish raw 0x50 payloads, shared parameters, factors, checksums derived from private device bytes, serials, MACs, ROM/SDK content, unique fingerprints or raw captures. Lab notes must mark external/integrated/TR revision and firmware variation as unknown unless independently identified.

## Findings and owner decision

The first F7 stage has an independent community source and deserves **hardware-derived / moderate-confidence** classification, scoped to that observation. The F1-controlled second stage and the exact active 64-byte response are **source-backed Dolphin behavior with incomplete independent hardware provenance**. This supports retaining the owner's **compatibility** value `0x02` while prohibiting a claim of challenge completion or physical fidelity. Revisit implementation only when a measured consumer demonstrably cannot initialize MotionPlus and a coherent, independently implemented challenge can be validated without source-code/key-material contamination.
