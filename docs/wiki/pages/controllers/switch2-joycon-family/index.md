# Switch 2 Joy-Con family

The [left](../switch2-joycon-left/index.md) and [right](../switch2-joycon-right/index.md) USB identities are distinct. SDL pair mappings are not evidence of a native combined device. [`switch2jc-pa2-per-half-host-units`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/switch2jc-pa2-per-half-host-units.json). Native USB consumers use vendor bulk startup alongside HID, while pinned Linux USB matching and SDL rejection of Bluetooth do not establish retail wireless behavior. [`switch2jc-pa2-usb-bulk-startup`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/switch2jc-pa2-usb-bulk-startup.json), [`switch2jc-pa2-bluetooth-consumer-gap`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/switch2jc-pa2-bluetooth-consumer-gap.json).

SDL source maps sideways and vertical modes and gyro/accelerometer orientation, but no mouse capture or actual mouse transport is established. [`switch2jc-pa2-sdl-controls-and-modes`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/switch2jc-pa2-sdl-controls-and-modes.json), [`switch2jc-pa2-sdl-motion`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/switch2jc-pa2-sdl-motion.json), [`switch2jc-pa2-mouse-consumer-gap`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/switch2jc-pa2-mouse-consumer-gap.json), [`switch2jc-pa2-mouse-device-unknown`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/switch2jc-pa2-mouse-device-unknown.json), [`switch2jc-pa2-native-transport-unknown`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/switch2jc-pa2-native-transport-unknown.json). All findings here are source-only or unknown.
