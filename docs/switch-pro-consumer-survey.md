# Switch Pro — public software-consumer survey (G)

**Research parity, part 2: consumers and decisions** · **source-only**, not run. Evidence reference: research main after A–F merged; virtualgamepad main `8f60dcce577ab02bd8ed8b3426349729d417393c` was reviewed for the acceptance-model split, not modified. Physical controller identifier: `057e:2009`. Consumer inventory ranked by likely acceptance value, **not** probability of successful recognition. Rank does not mean supported.

**Realization semantics:** `linux.uinput` creates evdev nodes only; `linux.uhid.usb` creates a virtual HID bus-visible device with a descriptor which might be bound by `hid-nintendo` or `hid-generic`, exposing hidraw and derived evdev. Neither represents a physical USB controller, RF or XUSB interface. `linux.uhid.bluetooth`, if declared, only presents BUS_BLUETOOTH metadata—not BlueZ pairing. `dummy_hcd`/USB/IP/composite audio is *not* interchangeable with UHID. No USB/IP audio realization is established for this controller; label not-supported/not-measured, never infer audio from controller HID.

**Family-specific sourced behavior:** SDL HIDAPI Switch reads feature/subcommand 0x80 USB handshake, 0x21 subcommand ACKs and 0x30 full IMU input; hid-nintendo owns handshake and creates evdev; joycond depends on hid-nintendo evdev and can create a separate virtual pad. Pinned claims and sources from part 1: `audio-linux-hid-nintendo`, `audio-sdl-switch-pro`, `parity-g-joycond-readme`. No physical Switch Pro on hand; Bluetooth RF pairing and real subcommand contention are No evidence.
