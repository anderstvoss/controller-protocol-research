# Decision brief — Wii Remote Plus integrated MotionPlus revisions

**Recommendation (October 27, 2026):** **Rank (a) a bounded, opt-in TR/Gen 2 compatibility prototype first**, not a universal or hardware-faithful Plus implementation: add a distinct curated `WiiRemoteRevision::Plus` candidate, Nintendo `057e:0330`, `Nintendo RVL-CNT-01-TR`, an **always physically present but logically deactivate-able** MotionPlus, and reuse the existing external MotionPlus state/register/gyro model **as an explicit unverified approximation**. Require native kernel and app acceptance tests before release, and reserve early non-TR integrated Plus as an **independent unknown revision** (do not silently advertise it as either original or TR). **Rank (c) waiting for hardware** is preferred if a faithful physical specification is a hard gate. **Reject (b), Plus identity without integrated MP**, as an advertised complete Plus product: the kernel assumes built-in MP on Gen 2 and customers can reasonably require gyro.

This research is **source-only**; it does not authorize virtualgamepad implementation and performs **no host or hardware runs**. Starting main `84fa4a0ac43115e5856ce51b3a48c9980c71bcf0` has **174 Wii claims**, including merged [F7/challenge #49](wii-motionplus-challenge.md); [W9 PR #50](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/pull/50) may update main independently. Existing [Wii revision ledger](ledgers/wii-remote-l0.md), [Dolphin F6/40](wii-dolphin-attachment.md), [consumer comparison](wii-motionplus-consumer-comparison.md) and [challenge](wii-motionplus-challenge.md) remain authoritative within their proven scopes. No C5/W-series source claim was rewritten.

**Gates for (a):** precise native identity and SDP metadata, a valid original/caller-imported calibration policy (never invented retail data), no attached-extension false positives, MP-only kernel/xwiimote and Dolphin gyro acceptance, stable modes 04/05/07 and hot-plug on simulated downstream accessory, isolation from original RVL-CNT-01 and no regression to F6/40 or F7 compatibility. Hardware fidelity remains *unknown* until a physical capture.

## 1. Physical revisions, labels and Bluetooth identities

The **plastic regulatory/model marking** is distinct from the **Bluetooth SDP name** and **HID VID/PID**. Nintendo's own [identification guidance](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/2956/~/how-to-identify-a-wii-remote-plus) distinguishes original **RVL-003** from Plus **RVL-036** by a bottom model label and MotionPlus-inside marking. Its [2010 announcement](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/News/2010/Wii-Remote-Wii-MotionPlus-Wii-Remote-Plus--252654.html) confirms a built-in sensor, unchanged use of Nunchuk/Classic attachments and launch colors white, black, pink and blue; marketing functional equivalence is **not byte-for-byte register identity proof**.

| Hardware family | Physical model / observed assembly | Bluetooth SDP name | HID vendor:product | Evidence strength |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Original Remote, **no built-in gyro** | RVL-003 | `Nintendo RVL-CNT-01` | `057e:0306` | Established consumer identifier, not unique proof of sensor absence from any arbitrary unit |
| **Early Wii Remote Plus** (Gen 1.5, non-TR) | RVL-036; e.g. documented early white/black board/revision variants | **Same** `Nintendo RVL-CNT-01` | **Same** `057e:0306` | [wiiuse hardware matrix](https://github.com/wiiuse/wiiuse/blob/e769bd73860a4f941bd110ebae5a790f968b71e3/README.mkd#L103-L112), [xwiimote board samples](https://github.com/xwiimote/xwiimote/blob/4df713d9037d814cc0c64197f69e5c78d55caaf1/doc/DEVICES#L316-L335), [xwiimote type guidance](https://github.com/xwiimote/xwiimote/blob/4df713d9037d814cc0c64197f69e5c78d55caaf1/lib/xwiimote.h#L99-L115); actual unit remains unmeasured |
| **Later Wii Remote Plus** (Gen 2 / TR) | RVL-036; documented later cyan revision alongside other finishes | `Nintendo RVL-CNT-01-TR` | `057e:0330` | [WiiBrew SDP table](https://wiibrew.org/wiki/Wiimote), [xwiimote Bluetooth trace](https://github.com/xwiimote/xwiimote/blob/4df713d9037d814cc0c64197f69e5c78d55caaf1/doc/DEV_REMOTE#L70-L84), [kernel name/PID matcher](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-core.c#L811-L854) |
| Separate **Wii U Pro** | WUP-005 | `Nintendo RVL-CNT-01-UC` | Not included as Remote Plus | Distinct consumer category. [Kernel classifier](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-core.c#L829-L841), [Cemu exclusion](https://github.com/cemu-project/Cemu/blob/29f2f1fae4631e2317977debd9eacd55cc28dd7d/src/input/api/Wiimote/hidapi/HidapiWiimote.cpp#L32-L40) |

The phrase **“early integrated RVL-CNT-01-TR” is not a supported generic category**: the documented early integrated Gen 1.5 family can bear the **non-TR** name/PID; TR denotes the later identity. `gen15` is an illustrative xwiimote **device-type string**, not a stable manufacturer USB ID; [xwiimote header](https://github.com/xwiimote/xwiimote/blob/4df713d9037d814cc0c64197f69e5c78d55caaf1/lib/xwiimote.h#L99-L116). The revision/PCB markings in [xwiimote DEVICES](https://github.com/xwiimote/xwiimote/blob/4df713d9037d814cc0c64197f69e5c78d55caaf1/doc/DEVICES#L316-L335) are illustrative samples, not a complete Nintendo inventory. Physical **color, regional packaging and SKU** alone are not proven firmware/identity discriminators. More manufacturing variants or clone devices are possible, but their specific identity/register differences are **unknown**. Do not invent a complete color-by-PID matrix.

## 2. Consumer detection policy (do not conflate discovery with gyro)

- **Linux `hid-wiimote` v6.12:** binds `057e:0306` **and** `057e:0330` over HID Bluetooth; prioritizes recognizable device *name* over VID/PID when assigning Gen 1/Gen 2, with separate fallback on Nintendo PID. Gen 2 module table includes `WIIMOD_BUILTIN_MP`; Gen 1 does not, even if the underlying Gen 1.5 physical unit has MP. [match](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-core.c#L1867-L1876), [classifier](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-core.c#L811-L862), [module table](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-core.c#L570-L605). The builtin module sets a flag to **suppress MP hotplug polling**, not to prove a particular MP register layout. [module](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-modules.c#L2630-L2666), [poll policy](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-core.c#L868-L927).
- **SDL Wii HIDAPI:** both Nintendo PIDs are accepted by [VID/PID filter](https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/877399b2b2cf21e67554ed9046410f268ce1d1b2/src/joystick/hidapi/SDL_hidapi_wii.c#L182-L191) and [constants](https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/877399b2b2cf21e67554ed9046410f268ce1d1b2/src/joystick/usb_ids.h#L92-L98). Actual MP presence is tested separately by active mapped extension identity and inactive `A600FE` discovery; [MP state](https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/877399b2b2cf21e67554ed9046410f268ce1d1b2/src/joystick/hidapi/SDL_hidapi_wii.c#L385-L422). On Linux SDL deliberately delegates MP activation to the kernel [mode control](https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/877399b2b2cf21e67554ed9046410f268ce1d1b2/src/joystick/hidapi/SDL_hidapi_wii.c#L457-L481). PID recognition is therefore **not evidence of live gyro**.
- **Dolphin HIDAPI:** discovers by Nintendo name prefix **or** accepted `057e` PIDs `0306/0330`; [source](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteReal/WiimoteReal.cpp#L1012-L1037). Its emulated MotionPlus is programmed to allow disconnection and therefore sets a non-integrated first identity byte, even though comments distinguish integrated behavior. [emulator](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L95-L113). Dolphin emulator **does not provide retail Plus register validation**.
- **Cemu native:** HIDAPI enumerates both PIDs; [native HID path](https://github.com/cemu-project/Cemu/blob/29f2f1fae4631e2317977debd9eacd55cc28dd7d/src/input/api/Wiimote/hidapi/HidapiWiimote.cpp#L5-L47). Bluetooth pairing UI recognizes both names; [pairing path](https://github.com/cemu-project/Cemu/blob/29f2f1fae4631e2317977debd9eacd55cc28dd7d/src/gui/wxgui/input/PairingDialog.cpp#L229-L238). Its separate MP discovery requests inactive identity; [provider](https://github.com/cemu-project/Cemu/blob/29f2f1fae4631e2317977debd9eacd55cc28dd7d/src/input/api/Wiimote/WiimoteControllerProvider.cpp#L925-L950). Prior corpus documents an unresolved source-level gyro export/visitor issue, so *enumerated* Cemu Plus is not automatically a functioning gyro device.
- **wiiuse:** describes Gen 1, Gen 1.5 and Gen 2 separately and accepts a special **builtin MP identity code** in its inactive-MP probe; [README](https://github.com/wiiuse/wiiuse/blob/e769bd73860a4f941bd110ebae5a790f968b71e3/README.mkd#L103-L112), [probe](https://github.com/wiiuse/wiiuse/blob/e769bd73860a4f941bd110ebae5a790f968b71e3/src/motion_plus.c#L42-L80), [ID constants](https://github.com/wiiuse/wiiuse/blob/e769bd73860a4f941bd110ebae5a790f968b71e3/src/wiiuse_internal.h#L227-L242). Linux wiiuse uses native BlueZ/L2CAP rather than UHID, so the current virtual platform cannot claim positive wiiuse acceptance. [existing transport claim](../records/claim/wii-gap-consumer-wiiuse-bluez-l2cap-linux.json).
- **xwiimote / xwiishow:** gets kernel `devtype` and evdev children instead of running its own raw MotionPlus register controller. Its documented Gen 1.5 and Gen 2 categories reflect **kernel-driven classification**, not an independent retail identity oracle. [xwiimote API notes](https://github.com/xwiimote/xwiimote/blob/4df713d9037d814cc0c64197f69e5c78d55caaf1/lib/xwiimote.h#L90-L121), [kernel sysfs type](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-core.c#L1699-L1732).

**Most important discriminator:** `057e:0306` does not determine gyro presence; explicit inactive MP response is a separate discovery axis. Conversely `057e:0330` provides a strong **consumer Gen 2 identity** but cannot stand in for verified MP data.

## 3. Integrated MotionPlus and downstream accessories

| Item | External add-on evidence | Integrated Plus evidence / remaining unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical presence and logical activation | External MP can physically attach/remove; normal MP discovery at `A600FA`, activation `A600FE = 04` | Built-in Plus is marketed **permanently present**; it can be logically *inactive* or have mapping disabled, but cannot be physically unplugged from its Remote. Kernel suppresses *hot-plug polling* for Gen 2 rather than prohibiting MP mode switching. [kernel](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-modules.c#L2630-L2666); real disable/write behavior by revision **not measured** |
| Inactive MP identity `A600FA/6` | [WiiBrew historical source](https://wiibrew.org/w/index.php?title=Wii_Motion_Plus&oldid=120079) documents `00 00 A6 20 00 05` on external MP | wiiuse includes special builtin inactive selector `A4200005` under its `A600FA` constants [source](https://github.com/wiiuse/wiiuse/blob/e769bd73860a4f941bd110ebae5a790f968b71e3/src/wiiuse_internal.h#L227-L242); Dolphin emulator says its first identity byte can distinguish integrated state but forces non-integrated for its detachable emulated model [source](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp#L95-L108). **Exact six bytes for early Plus and TR are not independently measured and must not be chosen as a universal literal.** |
| Active identities and modes | Mapping `04` stand-alone, `05` Nunchuk, `07` Classic; active `A400FA` mode identity. [WiiBrew](https://wiibrew.org/w/index.php?title=Wii_Motion_Plus&oldid=120079), [Linux modes](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-core.c#L491-L565) | Linux and wiiuse **reuse** external-style mode logic for integrated detection; this supports a **software compatibility hypothesis**, not a per-revision physical equality claim |
| Downstream Nunchuk/Classic | External MP pass-through interleaves gyro and modified accessory packets; [WiiBrew](https://wiibrew.org/w/index.php?title=Wii_Motion_Plus&oldid=120079) | Nintendo says **the same accessories can plug in** to Plus; kernel maps mode 05 for Nunchuk and 07 for Classic based on normal downstream ID, and can maintain separate extension nodes. Exact integrated accessory-bit behavior **unverified** |
| MP report `extension_connected` bit | Bit 0 in gyro byte 4 signals *downstream accessory connected*, not MP's own existence. [WiiBrew](https://wiibrew.org/w/index.php?title=Wii_Motion_Plus&oldid=120079), [Dolphin data format](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/3eb30c7620b4475b2e0404228ba0d2b238a132f1/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.h#L77-L115) | With no attached accessory, expected **false** while built-in MP remains present. This is a **source-inferred shared report-field semantic**, not a captured Plus packet. The kernel MP device/presence flag is a *separate* state from the downstream-connected bit |
| Status change on activation | External activation can alter extension-plugged status; [WiiBrew](https://wiibrew.org/w/index.php?title=Wii_Motion_Plus&oldid=120079) | Exact status-report transitions, debounce and timing for integrated Plus **unknown**. Kernel uses its general extension hotplug state machine, not a physical proof of equal status timing |

The source-backed **common user experience** (“same as Wii Remote + external MotionPlus”) is not sufficient to freeze chip-level semantics. Treat any incoming unusual active/inactive identity or missing/calibration bank as a possible revision difference, not automatically a corrupted device.

## 5. Other remote-side protocol differences

- **Bluetooth / pairing / transport is a confirmed major TR compatibility boundary.** [WiiBrew's SDP and HID notes](https://wiibrew.org/wiki/Wiimote) distinguish old and newer identities, an **authentication-before-interrupt/data L2CAP** requirement for TR, and the failure of legacy control-channel `SET_REPORT` on TR. xwiimote's pinned Bluetooth traces describe **Broadcom** on early Remote/Plus and **CSR** on its later sample [source](https://github.com/xwiimote/xwiimote/blob/4df713d9037d814cc0c64197f69e5c78d55caaf1/doc/DEV_REMOTE#L36-L85). Chip vendor and SDP class are **sample and consumer observations**, not invariants of every physical unit.
- **HID report IDs, extension data and base controls:** Linux's Gen 1 and Gen 2 module lists share keys, rumble, battery, LEDs, accel and IR; Gen 2 adds the built-in MP handling [source](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/hid/hid-wiimote-core.c#L570-L605). This strongly suggests **consumer-level reuse**, but not bit-identical SDP HID report descriptors or EEPROM images. SDL also shares one Wii HIDAPI driver for both PIDs [source](https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/877399b2b2cf21e67554ed9046410f268ce1d1b2/src/joystick/hidapi/SDL_hidapi_wii.c#L182-L191).
- **Descriptor, EEPROM, speaker, IR, rumble, LEDs, status flags:** No per-revision retail HID descriptor comparison, report-cadence trace, EEPROM layout dump, speaker gain/ADPCM validation, IR timing, or button/LED/rumble response comparison was obtained. Record **unknown for each** rather than claiming identical physical implementation. Known *consumer code paths* overlap, and WiiBrew documents common report families [source](https://wiibrew.org/wiki/Wiimote).
- **Colors/regions:** Nintendo's 2010 color and accessory announcement and xwiimote early white/black/later cyan samples establish examples, not a rule that all colors or markets imply a given Bluetooth chipset or identity. The Remote Plus body still uses the standard extension connector for Nunchuk/Classic; **none of the selected consumers keys driver capability directly on housing color**.

## 6. Smallest decisive physical experiment (**not run**)

**Authorization and comparison units:** one owner-authorized physical **early integrated RVL-036 (non-TR)** and one **TR RVL-036**, plus an **original RVL-003 + external MotionPlus** control, all with the **same original Nunchuk** where safely compatible; optional Classic in a separate bounded arm. If only one Plus revision is available, **stop at that revision** and do not promote the result to the other.

1. **Static identity, no personal data publication:** record licensed body model and physical revision class, *coarse* documented hardware marking (do not publish unit-unique markings), Bluetooth SDP name, vendor/product, service/class, relevant descriptor **length/report-ID presence table and equality flags** against control. Retain all raw SDP bytes and personal BT addresses locally only. Check sync via 1+2 and sync button with authorized adapter; record pairing/auth-before-data and status outcome.
2. **Baseline unconnected accessory:** with no downstream attachment and no external MP on a Plus, read inactive `A600FA/6`, `A60020/32`, check source format/CRC validity privately, sample `A600F7/1` over time, and status. Observe MP presence separately from the **downstream-connected** gyro flag after mapping `A600FE=04`; read active `A400FA/6`, gyro stream type and read/ACK errors. Compare state/timing against original Remote + external MP under the same steps.
3. **Nunchuk arm:** initialize same Nunchuk native mode before MP activation, note `A400FA/6` identity and `A40020/16` **only as private comparison input**. Activate MP mode `05`; read `A400FA/6`, `A400F6/4`, `A400F7/1` and `A40040/16`, and classify gyro vs interleaved Nunchuk reports with connected flag. Test one clean detach/reattach and return to mode `04`. Compare F6 fields and A40040 to inactive downstream values via **booleans only**.
4. **Classic optional arm:** separately activate mode `07`, check basic active ID, downstream flag, reported controls and no accidental state carry-over. Only expand if the first Nunchuk arm succeeds.
5. **Challenge and control behavior:** follow [#49's bounded F7/F1/0x50 plan](wii-motionplus-challenge.md) for each tested revision, **without publishing raw challenge bytes or factors**. Record only states, sample timings, read success/error and nondisclosing presence/change comparisons; no game ROMs, keys or private materials.
6. **Normal remote regressions:** compare report-ID set/lengths, short base EEPROM read behavior (do not save data), status/ACK, speaker setup ACK, IR init/mode reply, LED, rumble and battery/status flags. Verify original controller and TR/Plus do not get incorrectly reclassified on reset. No destructive writes or calibration modifications.
7. **Interpretation:** mark *measured physical revision* versus *inferred consumer behavior* per result; log unknown firmware. Repeat independently, not pooled. Present timing distributions and pass/fail states; one sample never establishes manufacturing universality.

**Safe published artifact:** sanitized matrix of hardware family, Bluetooth name/PID, source reference, mode, read offset/length, success or error, normal/Plus register equality yes/no, CRC validity boolean, downstream-flag behavior, selected report-ID membership and latency range. **Never publish** raw HID/SDP/register/EEPROM dumps, MotionPlus UID, full PCB/serial labels, Bluetooth addresses, calibration payloads, cryptographic challenge bytes, console keys or unique sample hashes.

## Coordinator decision / scope

**This PR is evidence only.** Require the coordinator to select **(a) opt-in TR compatibility engineering** or **(c) physical validation before model revision**, not an accidental firmware-faithful claim. For (a), keep Bluetooth HID identity distinct from immutable `has_integrated_motionplus` semantics; distinguish *physical presence* from *active mapped mode*, *downstream attached*, and *host gyro configured*. Do not generalize TR PID to early non-TR Plus. No change to virtualgamepad, C5/W-series records, existing controller manifests, or consumer-result experiments in this research PR.
