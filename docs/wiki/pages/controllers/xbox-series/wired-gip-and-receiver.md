## Wired USB is not ordinary HID

The pinned xone driver matches a vendor-specific wired GIP USB interface, receives an announcement, sends identify requests and registers gamepad or headset clients from returned metadata. Its parser handles ACKs, sequence numbers and segmented messages. Host-side timing choices must not be promoted into measured device deadlines. [xbox-xone-wired-bind](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-wired-bind.json), [xbox-xone-startup-identify](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-startup-identify.json), [xbox-xone-ack-sequence](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-ack-sequence.json), [xbox-xone-host-rejection](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-host-rejection.json).

The existing `xpad` evdev reference has **26 capabilities** in its recorded profile: 12 keys, eight absolute axes and six force-feedback capability entries. An xone gamepad that advertises Share can present similar functional inputs, but has a different input bus/identity and conditions Share on GIP metadata. This is not a GIP packet fixture. [xbox-series-xpad-evdev-shape](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-series-xpad-evdev-shape.json), [xbox-xone-input-share-guide](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-input-share-guide.json), [xbox-xone-evdev-compare](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-evdev-compare.json).

## Distinct outputs and accessories

- **Main versus trigger rumble:** xone builds a four-motor GIP output envelope but ordinary Linux `FF_RUMBLE` populates only the two main amplitudes. SDL's separate GIP HIDAPI path contains conditional independent trigger commands. Neither demonstrates actual retail impulse actuation. [xbox-xone-rumble-main-only](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-rumble-main-only.json).
- **LED/battery/power:** xone interprets status and publishes power/LED controls; the physical brightness and battery cadence are unmeasured. [xbox-xone-led-battery-power](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-led-battery-power.json).
- **Headset audio:** for an advertised GIP headset class, xone can register ALSA playback and capture, use GIP audio message paths and optional wired isochronous USB transport. Stock `xpad` instead disables that GIP audio interface. These are *different consumers*, not a contradiction or proof of physical sound. [xbox-xone-gip-headset](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-gip-headset.json), [audio-xbox-series-xpad-disables-gip-audio](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/audio-xbox-series-xpad-disables-gip-audio.json).
- **Proprietary Xbox Wireless Adapter:** xone binds a receiver and associates independent GIP clients behind it. This is not Bluetooth HID and does not determine the Steam Puck's slot semantics. [xbox-xone-receiver-bind](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-receiver-bind.json), [xbox-xone-receiver-clients](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-receiver-clients.json).

## What is still required

A real wired hardware reference must establish descriptor, startup/ACK replies, Share/Guide, four-motor output and headset behavior. A receiver needs independent pairing/lifecycle evidence. The source corpus explicitly retains these device-side unknowns: [xbox-series-usb-device-gaps](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-series-usb-device-gaps.json), [xbox-xone-physical-unknown](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-xone-physical-unknown.json).
