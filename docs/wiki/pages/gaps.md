## How to close a gap

File a specific claim/experiment with controlled procedure and privacy-reviewed fixtures in the corpus, then request a **manual wiki refresh**. Do not amend a wiki page first and later retroactively promote a claim. See [updating](updating.md).

## Recently documented source-only distinctions

- [Steam Controller BLE](controllers/steam-controller-2026/bluetooth-and-uhid.md) — consumer evidence only; the ATT MTU arithmetic is conditional, not a measured radio link.
- [Switch Pro HOME and NFC](controllers/switch-pro/home-light-and-nfc.md) — four player indicators plus one distinct HOME LED; NFC/MCU report codes remain controller-family context.
- [Xbox Series wired GIP](controllers/xbox-series/wired-gip-and-receiver.md) and [Bluetooth/xpadneo](controllers/xbox-series/bluetooth-and-xpadneo.md) — distinguish GIP headset, Bluetooth HID, xpad and keyboard F12 Share.
- [Controller/audio association](audio-association.md) — no demonstrated automatic pairing of a UHID-only controller with unrelated ALSA/PipeWire nodes.

All of these are *research follow-ups*, not newly completed device acceptance tests.

## November 11 research boundary changes

- [Xbox Series physical reference](controllers/xbox-series/physical-observations.md) **narrows** previous unknowns: the captured 283-byte BT descriptor matched one source example, and stock xpad input/reconnect was physically validated at the evdev layer. Neither provides raw GIP or independent trigger-motor actuation. [`xsphys-bt-descriptor-match`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/e8cca52c51f76c95b72c3bf6d27a9c04d854e9e7/records/claim/xsphys-bt-descriptor-match.json), [`xsphys-xpad-input`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/e8cca52c51f76c95b72c3bf6d27a9c04d854e9e7/records/claim/xsphys-xpad-input.json).
- [Puck receiver lifecycle](controllers/steam-controller-2026-puck/receiver-lifecycle.md) gives kernel/SDL *source* semantics, not a hardware Puck capture. Slot epochs are **inferred** and real pairing/bond behavior remains unknown. [`rcv-puck-reconnect-epoch-inference`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/e8cca52c51f76c95b72c3bf6d27a9c04d854e9e7/records/claim/rcv-puck-reconnect-epoch-inference.json).
- [Steam virtual kernel evdev](controllers/steam-controller-2026/virtual-kernel-evdev.md) establishes a bounded consumer experiment following a Menu mode switch; not physical-controller protocol fidelity. [`steam-d9-virtual-kernel-mode-gate`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/e8cca52c51f76c95b72c3bf6d27a9c04d854e9e7/records/claim/steam-d9-virtual-kernel-mode-gate.json).
- [Joy-Con Left](controllers/switch-joycon-left/source-and-consumers.md) separates raw fields from host transforms; source-only and no hardware sessions. [`joycon-cgap-left-sdl3-modes`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/e8cca52c51f76c95b72c3bf6d27a9c04d854e9e7/records/claim/joycon-cgap-left-sdl3-modes.json).
