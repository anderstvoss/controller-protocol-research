# Xbox One

Pinned stock xpad distinguishes USB `045e:02d1`, `02dd` and `02ea` from Series `0b12`; the older identities expose eleven keys without Share, eight axes and main-motor force feedback. USB GIP requires native interface startup, not just matching evdev capabilities. [`xboxone-pa2-usb-id-matrix`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/xboxone-pa2-usb-id-matrix.json), [`xboxone-pa2-xpad-evdev-no-share`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/xboxone-pa2-xpad-evdev-no-share.json), [`xboxone-pa2-xpad-wired-gip-boundary`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/xboxone-pa2-xpad-wired-gip-boundary.json).

Model 1708 Bluetooth IDs `02fd` and `0b20` have distinct kernel match entries. SDL source handles older firmware state/Guide/battery reports and descriptor-driven decoding conditionally; its Share exceptions cover these older IDs. xpadneo's documented BLE descriptor differences do not prove a physical Share control.
