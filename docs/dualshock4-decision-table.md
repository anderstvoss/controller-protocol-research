## Calibration provenance and fallback

**Calibration:** kernel hid-playstation accepts failed calibration with inaccurate IMU, old hid-sony aborts; SDL uses fallback scale (see E ledger). Any factory calibration must be caller-imported with provenance and physical firmware scope, not assumed to match canned +32000/-32000 generated by the wire helper. No actual DS4 is available.

**Policy recommendation:** maintain separate `compat` and `faithful` annotations per realization/consumer, with runtime caller-provided calibration only when provenance and device revision are explicit; a zero-filled/synthetic fallback is not evidence of actual physical calibration. Do not promote physical acceptance from a source-supported kernel parse or a virtual host run.

## Firmware, MAC, serial, revision and provenance

**Identity:** 054c:05c4 vs 09cc vs 0ba0 are not interchangeable. `0xa3` body 0x01 synthetic is an old-driver compatibility choice, **not authentic hardware revision**. Linux identity 0x12/0x81 policy varies by driver. No DS4 MAC/serial captured; wiki pending.
