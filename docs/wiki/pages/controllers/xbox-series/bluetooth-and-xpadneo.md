## Independent host paths

Microsoft Bluetooth HID Series identity **045e:0b13** is recognized in pinned kernel `hid-microsoft` and optional out-of-tree xpadneo sources; `hid-generic` may be a fallback. A source example describes a **283-byte** Series BLE descriptor, but firmware/revision parity is not established. SDL can choose descriptor-driven HIDAPI decoding or fallback Bluetooth report-ID logic depending on parse and backend selection. [xbox-cgap-linux-binding](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-linux-binding.json), [xbox-cgap-descriptor-example](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-descriptor-example.json), [xbox-cgap-descriptor-variants](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-descriptor-variants.json), [xbox-cgap-sdl-descriptor-gate](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-sdl-descriptor-gate.json).

**xpadneo is not stock xpad:** it can normalize the HID product/version, remap usages, and create multiple evdev children (gamepad, keyboard, consumer control, optional mouse). Thus a faithful event-layer clone of the stock xpad **26-capability** shape is not automatically faithful to xpadneo's outputs. [xbox-cgap-xpadneo-binding-identity](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-xpadneo-binding-identity.json), [xbox-cgap-xpadneo-multinode](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-xpadneo-multinode.json), [xbox-series-xpad-evdev-shape](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-series-xpad-evdev-shape.json).

## Semantics that can be misread

| Surface | Consumer interpretation | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share | xpadneo's `BTN_SHARE` **alias is `KEY_F12`**, typically emitted on a separate keyboard node; mouse mode can repurpose it | **Not** the stock xpad gamepad `KEY_RECORD` mapping — [xbox-cgap-xpadneo-share-f12](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-xpadneo-share-f12.json) |
| Guide | A default shift/modifier policy can delay or consume Guide events | Not native button timing — [xbox-cgap-xpadneo-guide-shift](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-xpadneo-guide-shift.json) |
| Axes | xpadneo exposes sticks, triggers and an extra `ABS_PROFILE` with its own fuzz/deadzone policy | Not stock xpad's complete evdev fingerprint — [xbox-cgap-xpadneo-extra-evdev-axis](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-xpadneo-extra-evdev-axis.json) |
| Trigger rumble | Bluetooth report `0x03` has four motor fields, but xpadneo derives trigger magnitudes from main `FF_RUMBLE` and trigger pressure | Not four independent evdev FF channels — [xbox-cgap-xpadneo-impulse-derived](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-xpadneo-impulse-derived.json) |
| Output delivery | In HOGP mode, xpadneo uses acknowledged HID `SET_REPORT`; a virtual UHID must answer the request | Not verified against an emulated device — [xbox-cgap-xpadneo-hogp-output](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-xpadneo-hogp-output.json), [xbox-cgap-xpadneo-uhid-set-report-reply](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-xpadneo-uhid-set-report-reply.json) |

Packaged xpadneo udev rules can restrict ordinary hidraw access, so SDL may see evdev rather than its direct HIDAPI decoder. This is a permission/consumer-selection question, not evidence of malformed device input. [xbox-cgap-xpadneo-udev-hidraw-gate](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-xpadneo-udev-hidraw-gate.json).

## Virtualization and physical limits

A Bluetooth-*bus* UHID object is a **host-local simulation**, not discoverable Bluetooth radio hardware. It is plausible for testing driver binding, decoder semantics and output/cleanup but has no completed acceptance run. Descriptor, firmware-specific buttons, battery cadence, pairing, independent impulse actuation and sound remain physical or consumer test gaps. [xbox-cgap-virtual-uhid-feasibility](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-cgap-virtual-uhid-feasibility.json), [xbox-series-bluetooth-device-gaps](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/ce6b8d3b70fd0957135eb921ef6098988f355cb1/records/claim/xbox-series-bluetooth-device-gaps.json).
