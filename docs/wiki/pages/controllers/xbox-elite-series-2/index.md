# Xbox Elite Series 2

Pinned USB `045e:0b00` uses xpad's GIP-oriented path; Bluetooth models `0b05` and `0b22` match HID Microsoft. The USB source advertises four grip keys, standard axes and no Series Share key; firmware-dependent reports and profile remapping can suppress individual paddles. [`elite2-pa2-usb-xpad-identity`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/elite2-pa2-usb-xpad-identity.json), [`elite2-pa2-bluetooth-hid-bind`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/elite2-pa2-bluetooth-hid-bind.json), [`elite2-pa2-xpad-paddle-evdev`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/elite2-pa2-xpad-paddle-evdev.json), [`elite2-pa2-xpad-firmware-paddle-paths`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/elite2-pa2-xpad-firmware-paddle-paths.json), [`elite2-pa2-xpad-profile-policy`](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/eb7887bf1aacc1b0baf6f5e973cac2ee41090c4e/records/claim/elite2-pa2-xpad-profile-policy.json).

SDL source uses different wired and Bluetooth paddle packet variants and remap guards, with grip-key mapping in evdev. xpadneo's optional profile axis and default-profile policy are not the stock kernel surface.
